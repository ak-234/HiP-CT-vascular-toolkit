Coronary lumen surface reconstruction · SDF pipeline

From a centerline graph to a watertight lumen.

coronary_sdf turns an Amira/Avizo SpatialGraph — vessel centerlines with a radius at every point — into a closed, CFD-ready surface mesh of the artery lumen, using a smooth-minimum capsule signed-distance field. Downstream tools then drive outlet flow boundary conditions and wall-shear-stress analysis.

16 pipeline stages 1 implicit field function-oriented watertight output

A coronary tree, after segmentation and skeletonisation, is stored as a graph of nodes (branch points, endpoints), points (dense centerline samples carrying a local radius), and segments (edges tagged with a Strahler order). The core idea of the package is to represent every vessel as a chain of tapered capsules and to define the lumen surface implicitly as the zero level-set of a signed-distance field built by smooth-min blending those capsules — so bifurcations get anatomically smooth fillets instead of the creases or spherical bulges a plain boolean union would leave, while the mesh stays watertight.

The logic behind it

Why a smooth-min distance field

Every stage exists to answer one question at each point in space: how far am I from the nearest vessel wall, and am I inside or outside? That scalar is the signed-distance field; the lumen surface is simply where it equals zero. Six ideas make it work.

1 · A vessel is a union of capsules

Each segment is split into short spans; every span becomes a capsule — a line segment (p₀,p₁) with radii (r₀,r₁), i.e. a cone with hemispherical caps. Distance to a capsule is cheap and exact; the lumen is their union.

→ capsules.build_capsules

2 · Smooth-min, not a hard union

A plain min of distances leaves a sharp crease. A log-sum-exp smooth minimum rounds it into a fillet; k is radius-adaptive, so the fillet is a constant fraction of vessel size.

smin = −ln( Σ e−k·dᵢ ) / k

→ smooth_min_exp · adaptive_smin_k

3 · Anti-bridge carve

Two vessels can be millimetres apart yet far apart in the tree. A distance-only blend would bridge them. Using the parent / child / sibling masks, the carve pushes the field back out wherever a non-adjacent rival intrudes — only connected segments blend.

→ evaluate_sdf · build_directed_topology

4 · Flat caps & carina taper

At a junction, hemispherical end-caps balloon into a ball. A flat cap truncates each incident capsule at the node, and an optional carina taper cones the last radii — so N vessels meet in a clean Y / T / X. Terminals get a planar cap for CFD outlets.

→ SDF_FLAT_CAP_BIF · taper_bifurcation_carina

5 · Narrow band for speed

The zero level-set lives in a thin shell around the capsules, so evaluating the whole grid is wasteful. The field is computed only for voxels inside the union of per-capsule bounding boxes, in batches, querying the nearest few capsules per voxel.

→ compute_grid · build_narrow_band

6 · Smooth first, or the wall vanishes

Raw skeletons are noisy and unevenly sampled. On a tight bend the swept tube self-intersects and the union deletes the inner wall; at junctions, stair-step radius jumps cut necks. Densify, bridge, B-spline-smooth (with a curvature limiter) and clean the radius profile before any capsule is built.

→ the smoothing module

Downstream tools

From surface to simulation

Built on the same parser and topology code, these turn the reconstructed lumen into CFD boundary conditions and analysis. Each is a runnable module (python -m coronary_sdf.<tool>).

flow_fractions
Outlet flow-split BCs

Van der Giessen diameter-law flow splits at every bifurcation, propagated to the terminal outlets and matched to boundary zones in an ANSYS .msh.

in .am.xml + .mshout flow_fractions.csv + CFX .ccl
resistance_based_outlet_BC
Pressure-outlet BCs

Resistance-coupled pressure outlets: each outlet's pressure tracks the live inlet flow via Ohm's law for fluids, using the Giessen fractions. Stable, near-uniform outlet pressure.

in flow_fractions.csvout resistance .ccl
epicardial_annotation · manual_prune
Annotate & prune

Annotate the main epicardial vessels once in a 3-D picker; they stay preserved while side branches are pruned at a series of radius ratios — or click branches to cut by hand.

in .am.xmlout pruned .am.xml + epicardial.json + surfaces
wss_postprocess · wss_contour_compare
Wall-shear-stress

Radially-resolved WSS along main vessels — worst / shielded / mean per station from a swept 90° arc window — and a shared-centerline segment sampler for paired statistical comparison.

in CFD wall CSV + modelout wss_*.csv / .png
tube_union
Alternative mesher

For very wide radius ranges (e.g. HiP-CT microvasculature): a capped tube solid per segment plus junction spheres, merged by a robust MeshLib boolean union. Cost is O(edges).

in .am / .xmlout single watertight .stl
region_vtk output
Coloured inspection

Every surface is written with per-face region_id, strahler, is_junction and bif_level tags for colouring in ParaView.

in lumen surfaceout lumen_bspline*_regions.vtk
